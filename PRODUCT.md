# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Nippon Toyota HR staff use the portal during daily candidate screening and hiring work. Candidates use public application and pre-interview forms.

## Product Purpose

The portal replaces spreadsheet and email handoffs with one recruitment workflow. It helps HR screen candidates, send forms and interview instructions, review submitted information, and move candidates through hiring stages.

## Brand Personality

Professional, calm, and familiar. The interface should feel trustworthy during repetitive operational work.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative or flashy SaaS styling, novelty interactions, and visual effects that distract from recruitment tasks.

## Design Principles

- Keep the current task and next action obvious.
- Use familiar product controls and predictable interaction patterns.
- Present candidate information clearly without unnecessary decoration.
- Reduce manual handoffs and repeated data entry.
- Confirm consequential actions before they affect candidates.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, semantic controls, and reduced-motion preferences.
