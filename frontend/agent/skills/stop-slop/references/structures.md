# Prohibited Structural Patterns

## Binary Contrasts & Negative Listings
- "It’s not about X, it’s about Y" -> (State Y directly)
- "Instead of doing X, we should Y" -> (State "Do Y")

## False Agency & Vague Declaratives
- "The solution presents itself" -> (Name who found the solution)
- "The results speak for themselves" -> (State what the results actually are)

## Rule of Three Lists
- AI naturally lists things in triplets (e.g., "fast, reliable, and secure"). 
- Break these into two items, or expand to four to disrupt the predictable machine rhythm.
